## Cross-cutting facts

**札幌駅 boarding is outdoors.**
- The 札幌駅バスターミナル closed on 2023-09-30. It is planned to stay closed until FY2028 (令和10年度).
- Intercity buses board at outdoor street stops around 北3条広場 (アカプラ).
- The city points waiting passengers to free benches in チ・カ・ホ.
- Source: https://www.city.sapporo.jp/sogokotsu/kasetsubus.html
- **Indoor alternatives in Sapporo:**
  - 中央バス「札幌ターミナル」 (大通東1) is the 2nd stop on most 中央バス routes.
  - わっかない号 boards at 大通バスセンター1階のりば.

**Parsing (the key finding for automation):**
- **中央バス — reliable.** Route detail pages (`/highway/index.cgi?ope=det…`, `/airport/timetable/?ope=det…`) are static server-rendered HTML.
  - Each stop is one `<tr>` with `<th class='Kbn'>` (乗/降), `<th class='BusStop'>`, and one `<td>` per trip.
  - They also carry an operator-per-trip row and `所要時間` / `共同運行会社` / `乗車方法` text.
  - The validity date is in the page header, e.g. 「2026年7月1日更新（夏ダイヤ）」.
- **北都交通 — parseable, with work.** Pages are static WordPress HTML, but stop names sit in a separate `timetable__header` table and times in a `timetable__body` table, so rows must be zipped by index. Each route has a PDF too.
  - Each route page shows live status text under 「運行情報 Operation」 (平常運行 / 一部運休).
- **北海道バス (ニュースター) — parseable, with care.** Static HTML, but the tables are transposed (one column per trip). They include 「(運休)」 trip markers and stale commented-out tables. The site is http-only.
- **道南バス — PDF only.**
